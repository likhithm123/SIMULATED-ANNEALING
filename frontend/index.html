📖 How Adaptive Simulated Annealing Works

1. The Analogy of Annealing

In metallurgy, heating metal allows atoms to wander freely, and slow cooling locks them into a durable, defect-free crystal structure. In algorithms, high temperature allows exploration (accepting worse solutions to escape traps), while low temperature exploits good solutions.

2. The Flaw of Conventional SA

Conventional SA follows a blind, predetermined cooling schedule (e.g., $T_{k+1} = 0.995 T_k$). Once it cools, it becomes rigid. If it gets trapped in a local minimum valley, it is frozen forever and cannot escape.

3. The Adaptive Feedback Controller

Adaptive SA acts like a smart thermostat. It monitors search health online: if stagnation rises above a threshold or diversity collapses, it reheats the system to leap out of traps, and cools aggressively when discovering fertile valleys.
